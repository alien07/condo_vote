# Questions for a lawyer

Open legal questions in one place, so they can be sent to a lawyer together.
Each one says what the app does today, so the answer can be turned into a
setting or a change. When a question is answered, write the answer and the
date here and in the decision it affects, then remove it from "Open".

Background: [compliance.md](compliance.md),
[resolution-rules.md](resolution-rules.md), [decisions.md](decisions.md).

## Open

### Quorum (Condominium Act section 43)
1. **Does a general meeting that misses quorum make its resolutions void?**
   Today the app shows "quorum met / not met" on the result, the committee
   page and the PDF, but each agenda item is still decided by its votes.
   (Added 2026-11-18.)
2. **Committee meeting quorum.** The quorum option "Committee policy" is not
   calculated today; the result says "not evaluated". Proposed: "committee
   meeting: at least half of the committee members attend", counted by
   members, not by ownership. Confirm the rule and the source.
   (Added 2026-11-18.)
3. **First general meeting.** The meeting type "First general meeting"
   (added 2026-11-18) uses the same quorum options as other general
   meetings. Confirm the quorum and any other rule that differs (for example
   the time limit after the juristic person is registered).
4. **Reconvened meeting.** The "second call, no quorum" option assumes the
   new meeting is called within 15 days of the first. Confirm, and whether
   the app should check the dates.

### Resolutions (sections 48 and 49)
5. Confirm the pass-rule presets for sections 48 and 49
   ([resolution-rules.md](resolution-rules.md)) before they are used for a
   real meeting.

### Abstain (D017)
1. **Must every agenda item offer "Abstain"?** Since 2026-11-18 the admin
   chooses per agenda item (off by default), and Abstain counts as voting
   in the pass rules. Confirm whether the law or common practice requires
   it on resolutions, and whether it should count toward "votes cast".

### Records and documents (D019)
6. Does the Land Office accept the printed meeting record?
7. Is a digital signature required on the record or the result?
8. May owners see per-room results, or only totals?

### Meeting changes (roadmap V2)
9. Notice period when a published meeting is postponed (setting, default 7
   days).

### Committee (D026)
10. **Committee positions and size.** The app offers chair, vice chair,
    secretary, assistant secretary, treasurer, member and a typed "other"
    position (user decision 2026-11-19). Only the chairperson is set by the
    Condominium Act; the other positions come from each building's
    regulations. Is any other position required by law? The Act caps the
    committee at 9 members: confirm the limit. The app does not warn above
    9 members today. (Added 2026-11-19.)
